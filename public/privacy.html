Privacy Policy: Mashnu AI Content Engine

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Operator: Mashnu AI, Bengaluru, India (mashnu.com).

The Mashnu AI Content Engine is an internal tool that Mashnu AI uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the channel owner.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the app requests only the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload). It is used solely to upload videos that Mashnu AI itself created. The app does not read, list, edit or delete existing videos, comments or analytics.

What is stored

An OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted secret in Mashnu AI's own GitHub Actions settings. The app collects, stores, shares and sells no personal data about any other person.

Third parties

Video scripts are drafted using Google's Gemini API. Videos are uploaded to YouTube and are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
